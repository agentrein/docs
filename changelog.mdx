<Update label="2026-10-10 — Intent Drift Detection" description="Intent Drift Detection">

## Intent Drift Detection

TEAM organizations can verify whether action results match a session's intent. Outcomes are reported as `VERIFIED`, `DRIFT_DETECTED`, or `UNVERIFIED`, with meaningful mismatches identified at a score `0.85` or higher. Learn more in [Core Concepts](/core-concepts).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-09 — Organization Policies" description="Organization policies">

## Organization policies

Create organization-level rules to allow actions, require approval, or block them. Use policies to apply `ALLOW`, `REQUIRE_APPROVAL`, and `BLOCK` decisions consistently across your agents; see [Policies](/sdk/policies).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-08 — GDPR session purge" description="GDPR session purge">

## GDPR session purge

PRO organizations can permanently purge an individual session to support GDPR data-removal workflows. See the [API Reference](/api-reference) for the session purge endpoint.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-07 — Agents management" description="Agents management">

## Agents management

Manage your agents through the REST API: create agents, view their details, update them, and delete them when they are no longer needed. See the [API Reference](/api-reference) for agents endpoints.

</Update>
